# Launch 3 (2026-10-05): larger boards, as an A/B on rollout length.
# Launch 2's stage at R 6 8 10 with lr 3e-4 made both models WORSE than the weights they started from
# (the larger model fell from 0.88 to 0.33 on bridges in 2,500 iterations), so: a much lower learning rate
# (5e-5, where stage 1 ended), the small radii kept in the mix, and a tighter rollback (a 15% relative drop
# in the held-out score reloads the best weights and halves the rate).
# Both runs start from the larger model's stage-1 best (hidden 256, 24 channels; it carried over to
# R 8-10 better than the small one). They differ ONLY in rollout length per iteration:
#   ha: 6R-10R steps, as before.      hb: 3R-6R steps (pool states persist, so settling can span visits).
