# Launch 10: the tap as a one-time event (docs/spectacle-nca-taps.md §4.6). Two arms of nca/strand/train3.py, each
# its own process on the one L4, both option E at channels 96 / hidden 256 / depth 2 (tap-e2-w's shape), warm-started
# from tap-e2-w-l3's best.pt (the doc's choice; the consts layout is train2's, so --init loads):
#   tap-i   --tap impulse: the tap's 59 planes are consts on its cell for one step, then zero (the product)
#   tap-f   --tap fixed: no tap planes ever; at the tap the host writes the cell's two chord edges and the code's
#           35-plane form into channels 61-95 (scalar at dirGroups 6) -- the diagnostic arm
# Targets from nca/strand/sim.py (merged, PR #24): tips one chord per two steps (decision 4), the tile rule's hits
# and both lines dying as waves (decision 3), absorption at own lines; sim.target_weight's windows (due from
# on + 2, don't-care from on - 1 and for 2 steps after off). Damage (decision 6): noise on line cells (p 0.3, sigma
# 0.1-0.3), the collisions and staggered taps of the episodes, and from T2 on a mid-episode tap on 10 % of kept
# slots. Stages per arm (startup.sh chains them with --init from the previous stage's best.pt and adds --schedule
# time: the lr, 5e-4, decays x0.3 at 60 % and x0.1 at 85 % of each stage's minutes):
#   t1l2 45 min   T1 persistence: one strand, one-shot tap, on level 2
#   t1l3 120      T1 on levels 2 + 3
#   t2   90       T2 collisions: 2-4 taps of different codes meeting inside the window, a quarter control pairs;
#                 a third of new slots still T1 (--remix). --hit-min 8: sim's collision draws hit at age 2 (median;
#                 87 % by age 7 at level 2, 69 % at level 3), before the lines have grown; with the first hit at 8
#                 or later the median hit is at 13-18 and the lines 18-38 chords long, so the dying waves and the
#                 fleeing tips are what is trained (measured on the Pi: 14-19 ms a drawn episode, ~2 a T2 iteration)
#   t3   60       T3 own-line meetings (joins, lines beside each other), the remix third T1 and T2
#   = 315 min a run + SETUP_MIN 25 = 340 min = 5.67 VM-hours; MAX_HOURS 6 is enough.
# Cost: ~$0.41/h (g2-standard-4 Spot, L4, disk and address) x 5.67 h = ~$2.32 (the doc's ~$2.3); a Spot reclaim
# adds a relaunch's setup (~25 min) and at most 500 iterations (--ckpt-every 500: <= ~2 min of t1l2, ~8 of the
# others), ~$0.25 each. Fallbacks (§4.6): two reclaims in one launch: drop t3 and cut t1l3 to 90.
