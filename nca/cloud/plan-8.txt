# iteration at l2 (some more small ops per step) and at or under C's time at l3 / l4; E-wide about twice C's.
# Watch secPerIter.

# x levels 2-4 at m1b, float32: 0.32 GB each) + ~0.4 GB of CUDA context each = ~18.7 GB, ~20.5 GB with 10% allocator
# slack, of the L4's ~22.5 usable (by the saved tensors alone ~12.5 GB). So batch 16 for all three: equal across
# the arms and with plan-7's C and big-C, so E vs C is the nets alone; batch 24 would be ~27 GB before slack. The
# quick check's read-out (eval batch 64, no gradient, after the window is freed) peaks at 0.6 / 0.9 / 0.8 GB.
