Inside, as a cellular automaton

Paint walls on the hexagon field, in any order, a piece at a time. Every region holding less than half of the open border fills: a walled-in pocket, or the shorter side of a wall from edge to edge. Each cell runs one rule on itself and its six neighbours, and the answer depends only on the walls there are now. Source on GitHub

The rule

The state is a stack of layers, one integer per cell per layer. Each step, every cell reads every layer through the same kernel bank: itself and its six neighbours (E, NE, NW, W, SW, SE). From those readings alone, one update gives the cell's new value in each layer. The engine runs that and nothing else. The only input is the wall layer; fill is the output, and the rest are hidden layers. N is the number of cells, P the number of border cells.

LayerUpdate, from the kernel tapsValues
outFixed: 1 past the edge, 0 on the board. A border cell reads its out taps to find prev, the tap just before its run of outside neighbours going anticlockwise.bit
wallInput: written by you, never by the rule.bit
idx0 at the east corner, else idx(prev) + 1: position round the border.0…P
cntopen + cnt(prev) (restarting at the east corner): open border cells so far.0…P
totAt the east corner cnt(prev), the total T; elsewhere tot(prev).0…P
lab, dLexicographic min-plus: min( (idx, 0) if open border, (lab_k, d_k + 1) over non-wall neighbours k ), with d capped at N. A region's label is its smallest border position; a label whose source is walled off counts up to N and dies.0…P or none; 0…N
parThe first neighbour with the same label and d − 1: a spanning tree per region, rooted at the label's source.direction or none
sopen + Σ s(children), children being the neighbours whose par points here: open border cells below.0…P
vAt the root (d = 0), 2·s < tot; elsewhere v(par).bit
fillnot wall and (lab = none or v).bit

Each hidden layer is a fixed point of its update, given the layers above it in the table. So the stack settles to the same answer from any starting state, and walls changed mid-way are simply absorbed. Press “Scramble state” to fill every hidden layer with garbage and watch it recover, or pick a layer under “Show” to watch it settle. The readout checks the settled fill against a fill computed over the whole board.
